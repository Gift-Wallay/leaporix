Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

Gift Wallay values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Shipping Address
  • Billing Information
  • Order History
  • Account Information
  • Device and Browser Information
  • IP Address
  • Cookies and Usage Data

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Process and deliver orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Verify your account
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send order updates
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember your shopping cart
  • Improve website performance
  • Analyze visitor activity
  • Enhance your shopping experience

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function properly.

4. Information Sharing

Gift Wallay does not sell your personal information.

We may share information only with trusted service providers necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Payment providers
  • Courier services
  • Technical service providers
  • Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as payment gateways, analytics providers, or authentication services. These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

7. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account (subject to legal requirements)
  • Update your account details

8. Children's Privacy

Gift Wallay does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Gift Wallay through the contact information provided on our website.

Thank you for choosing Gift Wallay. We appreciate your trust and are committed to providing a safe, secure, and enjoyable shopping experience.
